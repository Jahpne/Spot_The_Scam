A small open model (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Apache-2.0) runs on your phone, so your messages stay private. One-time download of roughly 0.5 to 0.8 GB. Use Wi-Fi. Without it, the checker still works with rules and the database.

Got an app file (.apk) sent on WhatsApp or by link? Check what it asks permission for before you install it. The file stays on your phone.

What we check

  • Messages: requests for your PIN, OTP, BVN or card details, fees to receive money, "safe account" and refund tricks, lookalike links, fake urgency, prizes, plus the AI's second opinion.
  • Links: shortened links, cheap look-alike domains, fake brand names, hidden "@" tricks, no HTTPS.
  • APK files: permissions that read SMS, watch the screen or draw over other apps.

What we do not check: website security certificates, how old a domain is, what a website contains, or an app's code. Those need a server or a paid API. Nothing here is a guarantee.
